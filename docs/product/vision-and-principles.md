# Vision and principles

**Owner:** Product
**Status:** Approved foundation; implementation not started

## Vision

Mosaic helps people developing hobbies, skills, creative pursuits, fitness goals, and other meaningful life areas easily capture the work they are putting in and turn those individual moments into a meaningful visual story of progress.

## Target user and job

**Decision:** The primary user is a person intentionally developing an area of life over time.

**Decision:** The exact job to be done is: “Help me easily capture the work I'm putting into something and turn those individual moments into a meaningful visual story of my progress.”

## Principles

1. **Organize around Pursuits.** A Pursuit is a long-running, intentional area of life and growth, not merely a goal, habit, project, post, or collection.
2. **Creativity in presentation. Simplicity in documentation.** Each Pursuit may tell its story in a fitting way; capturing sessions, notes, media, and other evidence should remain low-friction.
3. **Prove the concrete before extracting the general.** Golf is the first vertical slice. Shared concepts emerge only from demonstrated needs.
4. **Private first.** Pursuits and activity are private by default. Future sharing must be intentional and opt-in.
5. **Keep iterations coherent and reviewable.** Iteration 0A reviews a read-only story before 0B introduces practice capture.
6. **Respect honest data.** Summaries must be understandable and must not imply precision the product has not earned.

## Iteration 0 boundaries

**Decision:** 0A is an iPhone-primary, fixture-driven Golf visual journey. It includes read-only sample rounds as story evidence but no round-entry flow, capture, persistence, accounts, sync, or backend.

**Decision:** 0B adds a thin practice-session capture journey after the 0A information architecture and visual direction have been reviewed.

**Non-goal:** Iteration 0 does not deliver completed-round logging, official handicap calculation, social features, a universal Pursuit schema, or Pursuit creation/customization UI.

## Success signals

**Experiment:** In 0A ask, “After several months, can I see the story of getting better?” A reviewer should be able to answer using current focus, practice history, consistency, progress, sample rounds, media, and milestones.

**Experiment:** In 0B, verify that a user can easily record the agreed minimal practice session and see that moment become part of the Golf progress story.

**Assumption:** A Golf-native presentation will make progress more legible and motivating than a generic dashboard.

**Open question:** After the visual journey is understood, which behavioral signal should 0B optimize?

**Open question:** Which Golf summaries are useful and honest?
