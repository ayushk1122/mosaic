# Glossary

**Owner:** Product
**Status:** Approved foundation; terms may be refined through review

## Product terms

**Mosaic**
The product that helps a person document work within long-running Pursuits and see a meaningful visual story of progress.

**Pursuit**
A long-running, intentional area of life and growth. It is broader and longer-lived than a goal, habit, project, post, or collection. It may contain goals, milestones, activities or sessions, media, notes, and metrics.

**Golf Pursuit**
The first concrete Pursuit and Iteration 0 proving ground. Golf owns its domain language, records, rules, and presentation rather than inheriting a universal dashboard.

**Activity**
An umbrella product term for a piece of work or evidence within a Pursuit. It does not establish a shared persisted schema.

**Practice session**
The first planned capture workflow, introduced in 0B. Its exact fields and validation are not yet decided.

**Round**
A completed game of golf. Read-only sample rounds may appear in 0A as evidence in the progress story; entering rounds is deferred.

**Milestone**
A meaningful change or achievement made legible in a Pursuit's story. Exact Golf milestone rules remain provisional.

**Metric**
A quantitative summary derived from Pursuit-owned information. A metric must be understandable and honest; official handicap calculation is outside Iteration 0.

**Documentation**
In the product principle “Creativity in presentation. Simplicity in documentation,” documentation means capturing the work, moments, notes, media, sessions, and other evidence of progress—not writing engineering documents.

## Lifecycle terms

**Lifecycle**
The states and transitions describing a Pursuit over time.

**Decision:** Pursuits are long-running and need stable identity.

**Open question:** No lifecycle state set—such as active, paused, or completed—has been approved yet.

## Status terms

**Decision:** A settled direction.

**Reversible choice:** A selected approach intended to remain inexpensive to change.

**Experiment:** A hypothesis paired with a review or observable outcome.

**Assumption:** A belief that requires validation.

**Open question:** An intentionally unresolved matter.

**Assumption:** “Activity” may remain useful as conversational language even if features use more specific terms.

**Experiment:** During 0A review, verify that users understand Golf-native terms without needing generic Pursuit terminology on every screen.
